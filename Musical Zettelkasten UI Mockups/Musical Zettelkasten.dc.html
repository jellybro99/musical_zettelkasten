3musical·zettelkasten — arrangement

Where slips become a song. Search the slip-box on the left and drag slips onto the timeline — one is shown mid-drag with its drop marker. The arrangement owns a tempo, and any slip recorded at another tempo gets a fit badge (96→121) as it conforms. Clips can be repositioned (grip + drag), looped (right edge handle; loop repeats are marked by hairlines inside the clip), and varied — the open popover makes a variation of the Rhodes slip, transposed +3 with room to add notes. The song name has a randomizer. Static mock.

1musical·zettelkasten — first look

The idea I'm designing to: your audio snippets are catalog slips in a slip-box. You capture small loops and one-shots, file them, and later combine them into songs by dragging slips onto an arrangement. I've leaned the Classical system into that metaphor — slips read like index cards, waveforms are drawn as hairline gold strokes, and the arrangement assembles like a manuscript. Two screens in this first pass — the editor and arrangement arrived in later turns above. Everything is a static mock. Sample content is electronic/beats.
